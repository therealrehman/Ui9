Questions, bug reports, ideas, and privacy or data requests are all welcome.

In the app

Open Settings (long-press an empty spot on the Home screen > Settings, or long-press the launcher icon > Settings) and choose Contact us:

  • Email us – feedback, questions, ideas.
  • Report a problem – adds the app version, the Android version and the phone model to the email so we can find the bug. You can edit or delete them before you send.
  • Privacy question or request – anything about your data and the app.

These open your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Policies

  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Use

Both are also inside the app (Settings > Privacy policy / Terms of use / Permissions and data use) and can be read without internet.

Useful details when you write

Your phone model, Android version, the app version (Settings > OneUI 9 Launcher) and what you did just before the problem. A screenshot helps.
